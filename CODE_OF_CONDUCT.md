# Code of conduct

This project is maintained by Nextria and open to contributions from anyone.
We want the issue tracker, pull requests and discussions to be useful and
welcoming for database administrators, data engineers and developers alike.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique the code and the idea, not the
  person.
- Assume good faith. Many contributors work in regulated environments and
  cannot always share full details of their setup.
- Keep discussions on topic and give others room to participate.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change that conflicts with the
  project's constraints (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)), and that
  this is not a judgement of the contributor.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information, including data from their
  databases or Parquet files, without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to
the maintainers through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this
repository (it reaches the same people and is confidential), or contact
Nextria through [nextria.ai](https://nextria.ai). Reports are handled
confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and pull
requests that do not follow this code of conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently block contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate,
threatening or harmful.

This code of conduct is adapted from the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
